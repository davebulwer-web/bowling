Lane Legends

Ten pin bowling for 1 or 2 players
New game
Meter speed

Each ball takes four presses, like the old home-computer bowling games. Slide your bowler along the approach with ← → and press Space. Move the yellow arrow to the lane mark you want to roll over and press Space. Stop the power bar: more power is a faster ball that hooks less. Your bowler starts their run-up; stop the spin needle before they let go. Left of centre hooks the ball left into the 1-3 pocket, right of centre backs it up. ↑ ↓ switch between position and aim before you bowl. The pins are a physics simulation, so the same line can carry or leave a corner pin. Beat each opponent to unlock the next. Keys: Esc menu, 1 vs computer, 2 two players, P practice, M mute.
